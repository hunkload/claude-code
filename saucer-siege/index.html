HALCYON BAY

You are Krix, a Vorrn field harvester sent to soften up Earth. Halcyon Bay is defended by AEGIS, a corps of gene-boosted peacekeepers who leap rooftops and answer every disturbance with more firepower.

Knock out the three AEGIS Relays in each district to bring that district's Warden out of hiding. Defeat all three Wardens and the city is yours. Your skills grow with use: zap to boost Firepower, throw things to boost Psionics, dash and collect DNA to boost Agility, and fly the saucer to boost Saucer.
